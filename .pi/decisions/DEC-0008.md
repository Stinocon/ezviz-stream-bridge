# DEC-0008 — one shared on-demand upstream per camera

The fan-out is in MPEG-TS after the single remux, not inside the EZVIZ/VTM protocol. A late
consumer gets only a bounded tail and the live output. Slow consumers lose their own subscription
when its queue is full; they cannot pause the producer or another consumer. The upstream lifetime
is the interval from the first subscriber to the last subscriber, unless the operator explicitly
sets `max_stream_duration`.
